Welcome to Cricklewood Mosque

From daily prayers and weekend Quran classes to weekly community meals, we are a welcoming part of everyday life for people across Brent and North West London.

Brent & North West London

Five Times a Day, Every Day

Open all year for Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, plus Jummah, Tarawih and Eid prayers, with a dedicated hall for women.

Daily prayers

Growing Up with the Quran

An evening madrasah for ages six to fifteen, a ladies Quran class and a two-year Islamic Diploma Course for adults.

Education for all ages

Looking After Each Other

Free meals every Sunday, hot soup on Cricklewood Broadway every Thursday, and support through marriage and funerals.

Community care

Cricklewood Mosque, Our Mission

Our aim is simple: a caring, connected community rooted in the true Islamic values of peace, tolerance and coexistence.

Peace, tolerance and coexistence

Marriage, Funerals and Family Support

Cricklewood Mosque provides Islamic marriage services (nikah), funeral services and guidance for families during joyful and challenging moments in life.

Office team support

Education for All Ages

Children aged six to fifteen learn Quran, Tajwid and Islamic studies five days a week, while adults can study Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh and Aqeedah.

Ladies class every Wednesday morning
